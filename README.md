## What I'm Building

A research synthesis tool for UX researchers.

The idea came from a real gap I kept running into — knowing 
how to collect research but not always having clear support 
for what to do with it next: which insights matter most, 
how to frame the problem, which method to use at what stage. 
I had to research how to do research, and that shouldn't be 
the default.

The tool takes uploaded research materials and helps 
researchers prioritize insights by business impact, suggests 
next steps and methods, and supports problem statement and 
hypothesis framing. A second layer builds toward a research 
repository. A third is an ROI calculator — because research 
investment is often the first thing cut when teams can't 
quantify the return.
